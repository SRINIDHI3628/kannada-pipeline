Department: Directorate of Municipal Administration

1. Application for New Trade License
Eligibility: NA
Supporting Document:
1. Ownership Proof/Rental Agreement
2. Affidavit/Declaration
Application Fee : Variable Cost
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs 25
Delivery Time (Days) : 30
Procedure for applying:
1. Application submission (online, B1/K1 centers, CSC centers, AJSK centers, Bapuji Seva Kendras)
2. Operator verifies application and forwards application to health section
3. Health section verifies application and documents, do field verification and upload inspection report and forward application to admin
4. If Admin approves message to pay fee will forward to mobile and if rejects also intimation will forward to mobile
5. Applicant pays fee online
6. Admin digitally approves trade license

3. Building License
Eligibility: Building plan should comply Model Building Bye Law and Zonal Regulation.
Supporting Document:
Form-3/2, Sale /Title/ Gift deeds, Building Drawing / plan, Estimate Copy, Encumbrance Certificate, Affidavit,
Application Fee : Based on ULB
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Based on ULB
Delivery Time (Days) : 30 days
Procedure for applying:
Biulding License request submitted by the applicant will be processed by ULB officials with the technical opinion of Planning Authority / Development authority. After fee payment by the Applicant Building License will be issued.
