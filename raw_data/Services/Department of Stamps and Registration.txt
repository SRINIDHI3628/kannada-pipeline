3. Issue of certified copy of the certificate of Registration of Marriage under Hindu Marriage Act 1952
Eligibility: Bride Groom -21 Year
Bride -18 Year
Supporting Document:
1 . FORM 1 A
2 . MEMORANDUM
3 . JOINT PHOTOGRAPHS OF 2B SIZE
4 . AGE PROOF - both
5 . ADDRESS PROOF - both
6 . WEDDING CARD
Application Fee : 1. For Certified copies a. for every certified copy of marriage memorandum and identity slip, if any Rs.10 b. For every certified copy of application of form VII Rs.10 c. For every certified copy of order of refusal to file memorandum of marriage by Registrar. Rs.10 2. Miscellaneous a. For every application presented to the Registrar. Rs.10 b. For every search of a marriage memorandum. Rs.10 per year
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Certificate Fee each - Rs.10 + Scanning Fee
Delivery Time (Days) : 3 days
Procedure for applying:
1) Parties present before Marriage officer(MO) along with duly filled Application, Memorandum and other relevant documents.
2) Marriage officer will scrutinize the documents.
3) Bride and Bride groom present before Dealing assistant and sign the declaration form.
4) Pay scanning fee and marriage fee as applicable.
5) Marriage officer issues the certificate with Seal and signature.
6) Parties sign on acknowledgement and collect Marriage certificate.

8. Registration of Immovable Properties
Eligibility: Desire to Sale / Purchase
Supporting Document:
1.AFFIDAVIT AS PER ANNEXURE II
2. FORM NO 1 AS PRESCRIBED UNDER RULE
3 OF KARNATAKA STAMPS PREVENTION UNDER VALUATION RULES 19773. RTC OF THE LAND TO BE REGISTERED ISSUED 15 DAYS PRIOR TO PRESENTATION OF DOCUMENT IN CASE OF AGRICULTURE LAND
4. IN CASE OF AGRICULTURE LAND DECLARATION UNDER SECTION 81 A OF KARNATAKA LAND REFORMS ACT 1961
5.In case of agriculture land Permission or no objection certificate from the competent authority under section 6 of prevention of transfer of certain lands granted to SC ST Act 1978
6.IN CASE OF AGRICULTURE LAND SURVEY SKETCH AS PER FARM 11 E RED WILL RULE 46 H UNDER SECTION 131 OF KARNATAKA LAND REVENUE ACT 1964
7.Permanent account number or farm 60 or 61 where value of the property transferred exceeds more than five lakhs
8.In case non agriculture property Khata extract in case of BBMP Corporation CMC TMC Town Panchayat Gram Panchayat
9. In case non agriculture property Conversion order of the agriculture land for the non agriculture purpose along with khata and Tax assessment issued by the competed authority or approved layout plan by the competed authority
10.Grant certificates or possession certificate or sale certificate where Sites granted by Government or any authority or Departmen
Application Fee : 1percent Advalorem with a minimum of Rs.100
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : SD - 5% + ser charge
RF - 1% on the market Value
Delivery Time (Days) : 1 day
Procedure for applying:
1 Applicant has shall present the document to the Sub-Registrar along with the required records as per Govt Circular No.RD344 MuNoMu 2008 Date 06 April 2009 2 After scrutiny document will be issued to data entry operator for makeing the Data Entry and Capturing Photos and Thumb Impression of the Parties to the Document, Then signature of the parties will be obtained on the registration endorsement along with the signatures of the identifying witnesses 3 Document will be signed by the sub registrar and Office seal will affixed 4 Document will be scanned and saved 5 Document will be Delivered to the presentant
