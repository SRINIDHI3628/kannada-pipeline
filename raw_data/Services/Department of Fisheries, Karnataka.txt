Deparment: Department of Fisheries, Karnataka

1. Providing relief to distress fishermen
Eligibility: In case of death, boat loss/net loss and any medical expenses incurred
Supporting Document:
1.Photo of the deceased
2. Applicant Photo
3. Identity Proof
4. FIR COPY
5. Inquest Report
6. Postmortem Report
7. Police Case Diary
8. Police final Report
9. Consent letter to be provided when sanctioning relief amount to single person
10. Dependents details / santati nakshe
11. Newspaper Report
12. Original Purchases bill of Boat/net
13. Expenses incurred for repair quotation
14. Original bill of expenses
15. Doctors Report
Application Fee : Nil
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 40
Delivery Time (Days) : 90
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant fills the form and attaches the mandatory annexures
2. Department will process the application
3. Applicant will receive the monetary benefit

7. Issue of Licenses for fishing in Reservoirs
Eligibility: Fishermen by profession.
Supporting Document:
1. Address Proof
2. Applicant Photo
3 . Identity Card
4. Undertaking in INR 20 bond paper
5. Domicile Certificate for last 5 years
Application Fee : NA
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 25
Delivery Time (Days) : 10
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant logs into Seva Sindhu portal or approaches CSC operator.
2. Applicant/CSC Operator provides the user credentials
3. Applicant submits the application on Seva Sindhu portal/CSC Operator submits on behalf of applicant, along with necessary supporting documents and makes the payment for the service.
4. Applicant to provide clarification through re-submission of documents if requested by the Department
5. The approving authority approves and applicant collects the digitally signed certificate or the approving authority rejects and applicant collects the endorsement stating reasons for rejection

9. Application for Issue of license for purchase of kerosene to motorized fishing boats
Eligibility: Should be owner of Motorized fishing boat which has been registered in the Department.
Supporting Document:
1) Licence fee and challn details
2) caste certificate if applicant is SC/ST
3) Old kerosene permit details
4) Address proof and photo
Application Fee : NIL
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs.25
Delivery Time (Days) : 7 Days
Procedure for applying:
Apply with relevant documents in Sevasindhu portal
Submitted application will be scrutinized by Taluk Fisheries officer.
If found correct, will be resend to applicant to enclose fee paid details. After resubmission of application with license fee details by applicant, will be verified and approved by Taluk fisheries officer and forward to District officer for further approval .
