Department: Higher Education Department

1. Application for Duplicate Marks Card
Eligibility: Students
Supporting Document:
1. Affidavit on Stamp paper of Rs.20/- signed by a Notary
2. FIR Copy
3. Photocopy of old marks cards if available
Application Fee : Based on the University selected, the fees will be charged
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs.25
Delivery Time (Days) : 10 Days
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant needs to submit the application
2. Case worker verification in university
3. Superintendent verification
4. Verification by DR
5. Final Verification by Registrar
