Department: Directorate of social security and pensions

1. Manaswini Yojane
Eligibility: Women from BPL family aged above 40 to 64 years
Supporting Document:
1.Applicant Photo
2.Ration Card
3.EPIC
4.ID Proof
5.Residence Certificate
6.Age Certificate
7.Income Certificate
8.Address Proof
9.Bank Account(Passbook FirstPage)
Application Fee : NA
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 30
Delivery Time (Days) : 45
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant logs into Seva Sindhu portal or approaches CSC operator.
2. Applicant/CSC Operator provides the user credentials
3. Applicant submits the application on Seva Sindhu portal/CSC Operator submits on behalf of applicant, along with necessary supporting documents and makes the payment for the service.
4. The approving authority approves and applicant collects the digitally signed certificate or the approving authority rejects and applicant collects the endorsement stating reasons for rejection
