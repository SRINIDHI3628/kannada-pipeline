Department: Minor Irrigation Department

1. Technical Advice for the selection of well or bore well sites by Geohydrological method
Eligibility: NA
Supporting Document:
Address Proof
Certified Copy of Current Year RTC/ Khatha
Certified Copy of Land/Building Plan
No borewell in 500 mt vicinity certificate from
Grampanchayath\ PDO letter in rural area\ Ward
Engineer Letter from Urban Area
Application Fee : Rs 750 if type of land is agricultural land and Rs 1500 if type of land is non-agricultural land
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 20
Delivery Time (Days) : 45
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant fills the form and attaches the mandatory annexures
2. Department will process the application
3. Applicant will receive the certificate
