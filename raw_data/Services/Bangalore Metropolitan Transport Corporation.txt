Department: Bangalore Metropolitan Transport Corporation

1. Issue of Bus Passes to Physically Challenged
Eligibility: NA
Supporting Document:
1. Disability certificate and I.D card issued by Directorate for the Empowerment of Differently abled and Seniro Citizens
2. Proof of Residential Address
3. Aadhaar Card
4. Passport size photo
Application Fee : NA
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 25
Delivery Time (Days) : 15 days
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant shall fill the form & upload necessary documents to apply for this service
2.The department shall process the valid application
3. Once the service is delivered, the applicant is notified

3. Issuance of Student Bus Pass
Eligibility: Students
Supporting Document:
SATS: Student should upload the certified copy of the filled prescribed application.
Students without SAT Number : Should upload the current year fee receipt.
All students has to upload their Photo
Application Fee : Application Fee :
1st to 7th 150
8th to 10th - Girls -550 / Boys 750
PUC - 1050
PUC Evening college - 1630
Degree/Professional- 1300/-
Medical & Technical - 1830/-
Evening&PHD - 1630/-
All the classes SC/ST students - 150
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs.30/ online free
Delivery Time (Days) : 7 Days
Procedure for applying:
1. upload the prescribed application certified from head of the department from school from 1st to 10th
2. Fill the online application and upload the necessary documents.
3. the online application will be accepted or rejected after verification by the concerned nodal officer and final approval will be from BMTC
4. After approval the student must schedule the place of counter, date and time .
5. student can collect the pass from the scheduled counter.
After each step notification will be sent to the registered mobile number.

5. Application for Institution Registration BMTC
Eligibility: college nodal officer
Supporting Document:
NA
Application Fee : NA
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : NA
Delivery Time (Days) : NA
Procedure for applying:
For Institution registration, Institutions should upload the Authorization copy to run the Institution with the Nodal officer appointment letter (As per the prescribed format). After submission the said request will be farwarded to BMTC for verification, if approved the same will be pushed to Seva Sindhu to add in the drop down, if rejected then the institutions can re apply with proper information. After adding the institution/Course the related information/login credentials will be sent to the concerned institution through mail
