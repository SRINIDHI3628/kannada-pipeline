1. Prayojane_Sponsoring artist groups
Eligibility: 1)The applicant organisation must be registered as per Society act or Trust Act
2) The date of event must be at least 15 days from the current date
Supporting Document:
NA
Application Fee : Rs 30
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 20
Delivery Time (Days) : 3
Procedure for applying:
a) Application submission (Online, B1/K1 centres, CSC centres)
b) The application is routed to the Assistant Director at the respective district
c) Verification by the Assistant Director:
• If number of teams requested are less than four – Assistant Director approves or rejects the application request
• If number of teams requested are four or five – Recommendations are sent to the Director for review. Based on the Director’s review comments, the Assistant Director can approve or reject the application

6. Dhanasahaya Scheme towards financial support for Ph.D (Kannada) and M.Phil (Kannada) graduates
Eligibility: The applicant should not have received benefits for the same scheme in the same year
Supporting Document:
1)Photograph of the applicant
2)Photocopy of bank pass book
3)Caste certificate, if the applicant belong to SC/ST category
4) Photocopy of M.Phil/Ph.D certificates
5)Photocopy of the approval received by Guide towards publishing the thesis
Application Fee : NA
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 25
Delivery Time (Days) : NA
Procedure for applying:
1. Application submission (Online, B1/K1 centres, CSC centres, AJSK centres, BapujiSevaKendras)
2. The application is routed to District Assistant Director
3. Verification by the District Assistant Director. Recommendations of the District Assistant Director are sent to the Case Worker
4. Verification by the Case Worker. Review by committee
5. Approve or reject the application request by committee.
6. Dispatch of the GO/ endorsement by the Director
