3. Commencement Certificate for residential Dwelling houses / Apartments / Non-Residential Buildings
Eligibility: Owner with the help of Registered professional
Supporting Document:
1. Application for Commencement Certificate as in Form-V
2. Notice of Commencement of work as in Schedule-VI
3. Katha Certificate (Recent )
4. Katha Extract (Recent)
5. Upto date tax paid receipt.
6. Certificate of Sub Surface Investigation as in Form VI(A)
7. Certificate of Structural Safety in design (to be provided by SER) as in Form VI(B)
8. Photographs of Columns erected signed by owner and Registered Professional.
9. Plinth drawing showing the column position with setbacks and approved site plan.
10. Building Licence obtained.
11. Engineer/ Architect License Copy
12. Conditional documents prescribed during Plan Sanction, if any.
Application Fee : Depends on Guidance Value of Property
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Depends on Guidance Value of Property
Delivery Time (Days) : 30 days
Procedure for applying:
1. Submission of online application with relevant documents.
2. Receipt / Acknowledgment Distribution.
3. Location Inspection by Engineer.
4. Review of documents and maps.
5. Approval / Rejection by the competent authority.
6. Payment of fee for the applications approved by the competent authority
7. Issuing fee payment acknowledgement.
8. Approval for commencement certificate

5. Application for the Registration of New Trade Licence
Eligibility: 1) The applicant should be more than 18 years old
2) The applicant should not have any criminal records
3) The business should be legally permissible
Supporting Document:
1) Owner Consent Letter/Lease Agreement Copy
2) Electricity Bill
3) Occupancy Certificate (Non Mandatory)
4) Neighbor Concern Letter (Non Mandatory)
Application Fee : Business Logic
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs.30
Delivery Time (Days) : 30 working days
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant will submit the application
2. Department will review and process the application
3. Once reviewed and processed, applicant will receive the license/certificate
