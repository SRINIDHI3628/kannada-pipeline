Department: Karnataka State Archives Department

1. To provide copies of Mysore/Karnataka Gazettes, Proceedings and Historical documents which are preserved in Karnataka State Archives Department
Eligibility: Citizens
Supporting Document:
1.Aadhaar Card
2.Document xerox copy if available
Application Fee : 20 search fee + 3 Rs xerox fees per copy
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs.20
Delivery Time (Days) : 15 days
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant submits the application
2. AD will check if the requested copy is available or not
3. Caseworker searches the document.If available, applicant pays the prescribed fees
4. Verification and approval by AD
