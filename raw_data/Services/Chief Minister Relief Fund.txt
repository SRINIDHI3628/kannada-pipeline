Department: Chief Minister Relief Fund

1. Application for Relief Fund After Treatment
Eligibility: People from poor families or those who fall under BPL category
Supporting Document:
1) Applicant Photo
2) Final treatment Hospital bills from where the treatment was taken
3) Hospital Discharge Summary
4) BPL Card
5) Aadhaar Card
6) Bank Passbook first page Copy
7) Address Proof
8) Mobile number used by the applicant or their family members
Application Fee : NA
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs 35
Delivery Time (Days) : NA
Procedure for applying:
1) Applicant submits the application
2) Application verification by CMRF Caseworker (Hospital Verification)
3) Verification by Section Officers / Special Officers
4) Verification by Under Secretary / Special Officer
5) Verification by Joint Secretary
6) Verification by Accounts Officer
7) Send for DBT by Joint Secretaries for amount disbursement
