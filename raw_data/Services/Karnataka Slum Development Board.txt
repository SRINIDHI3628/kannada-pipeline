Department: Karnataka Slum Development Board

1. Application request to obtain a NOC to repair
Eligibility: Slum dwellers of declared slums
Supporting Document:
1. Voter PDS Photo
2. Parichaya Pathra
3. House Photo 1
4. House Photo 2
Application Fee : Rs.50
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs.20
Delivery Time (Days) : 30 days
Procedure for applying:
1. Submit the application with attachment
2. The application shall be processed by Excise Department
3. Applicant will receive information/output certificate when the service is complete
