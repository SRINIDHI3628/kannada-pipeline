Department: Bangalore Development Authority

1. Application for Possession Certificate for Sites
Eligibility: a) Site has been allotted by BDA
b)Lease Cum Sale Deed or Sale Deed Agreement is registered.
Supporting Document:
a) Scanned copy of Lease cum sale deed oe sale deed
b) Scanned copy of photo Identification proof & address proof
Application Fee : Rs 0
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs 25
Delivery Time (Days) : 7
Procedure for applying:
a) Application submission by allottee (Online, B1/K1 centres, CSC centres, AJSK centres, Bapuji Seva Kendras)
b) The application is routed to the concerned Deputy Secretary
c) Deputy Secretary forwards the application to the concerned case worker for verification
d) Case worker verifies submitted documents and application form. Updates the boundaries, office related details. Provides recommendations and forwards application to superintendent
e) Superintendent verifies application form, submitted documents and Possession Certificate content filled by case worker. Provides recommendations. Recommendations of the Superintendent are sent to the Deputy Secretary for review
f) Review by the Deputy Secretary. Approve or Reject the application.
g) Sign and Issue the Possession Certificate/Endorsement Letter

3. Application for New e-Khata
Eligibility: Property should be located in BDA limits.It should be either allotted by BDA or approved by BDA
Supporting Document:

BDA Allotted sites/ flats
1. Allotment Letter
2. Lease Cum Sale Deed /Sale Deed
3. Possession Certificate
4. Encumbrance Certificate (Form-15) till date
5. ID Proof
6. NOC from Bank (If availed loan on property)
BDA Approved Private Layout/Apartment
1. Layout/Apartment Plan Approval Order
2. Release Order
3. Possession Certificate (Not mandatory)
4. Allotment Letter (Not mandatory)
5. Lease Cum Sale Deed /Sale Deed
6. Encumbrance Certificate (Form-15) till date
7. ID Proof
8. NOC from Society (Not mandatory)
9. NOC from Bank (If availed loan on property)
Application Fee : Rs 0
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 40
Delivery Time (Days) : 15
Procedure for applying:
a. Application submission by allottee (Online, B1/K1 centres, CSC centres, AJSK centres, Bapuji Seva Kendras)
b. The application is routed to Revenue Officer
c. Revenue Officer forwards the application to concerned Revenue Inspector
d. Revenue Inspector verifies and forwards the application to the Manager for recommendations
e. Manager verifies submitted documents and application form and provides recommendations to Revenue Officer
f. Revenue officer approves or rejects the Khata creation, enters the fee amount and signs the certificate based on the documents verification
g. Applicant makes the payment for the approved Khata creation application requests
h. Khata certificate is issued online
