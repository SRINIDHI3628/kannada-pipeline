5. Application form to Establish a Regulatory Sandbox
Eligibility: Govt Depts/Pvt. Companies registered in Karnataka
Supporting Document:
1. Supporting Documents against authorised Regulatory Status
2. Authorisation Letter from Competent Authority
3. Supporting Documents against Description of Product, Service or Solution
4. Supporting Documents supporting Innovation in product, service or solution
5. Supporting Documents stating readiness of the prototype development 6. Evidence of Resources available to support testing
7. Business plan document for the product, service or solution
8. Supporting Documents against Legal or Regulatory requirements
9. Supporting Documents against Regulatory Status
10. Supporting Documents against Risks involved and Mitigation measures
11. CVs of Key Personnel (CEO, CFO, Directors)
12. Audited Financial Statement of last 3 years
Application Fee : NA
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 100
Delivery Time (Days) : NA
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant needs to Register in Seva Sindhu portal and Login to it
2. Applicant to Search and Apply for the Service Application to Establish a Regulatory Sandbox online
3. Applicant to tender the application processing fees through electronic transfer
4. Applicant to eSign the final Application during submission
5. Concerned Authority to process the application
6. Selected Applicant to be notified
