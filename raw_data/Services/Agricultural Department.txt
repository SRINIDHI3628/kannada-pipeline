Department: Agricultural Department
Source: https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in/

Services:

1. Issue of Manufacturing License for NPK Mixture Fertilizers
Eligibility: NA
Supporting Document:
1. Covering letter addressed to Joint Director of Agriculture (Fertilizer & Plant Protection), Head office, Bengaluru
2. Debiting of required fee of Rs. 2,000/- for each grade in the Khazane-2 (Challan)
3. Bank Guarantee for Rs. 50,000/-
4. Declaration of Laboratory facilities and Equipment’s as per Cl.21A of FCO, 1985
5. Certificate for Nomination as Responsible person
6. Detailed bio-data of the Responsible person (Name, Age, Qualification, Designation, Present & Permanent address, Phone Number-Office& Mobile, Experience etc.,)
7. Qualified person Degree certificate and Marks Card [B.Sc.(Agri) or B.Sc. with Chemistry as optional subject
8. Qualified person Appointment letter
9. Acceptance of the appointment order by the Chemist/Qualified person
10. Registration certificate from Industries & Commerce Department (SSI Certificate)/Certificate of Incorporation as per Companies Act/Certificate from District Industries and Commerce
11. Udyog Aadhar Certificate
12. Copy of Wholesale Fertilizer Dealer license
13. Firm Registration certificate
14. G.S.T. Registration Certificate & Assessment
15. Affidavit copy of Declaration regarding Responsible person as per Cl.24 of F.C.O. 1985
16. Enclose copy of Aadhar card of Responsible person
17. Sources of Raw materials - FORM – “O’’
18. Details of packing and marking on the container
19. Details of staff position
20. Production Capacity
21. Route Map of the Manufacturing unit
22. Invoice/Bills regarding purchase of equipment
23. Copy of project report
24. Proprietor deed / Partnership deed of Pvt. Ltd. Company / Memorandum and Articles of Association
25. Work sheet details for mixtures of manufaturing components
Application Fee : NA
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 100
Delivery Time (Days) : 55
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant fills the form and attaches the annexures
2. Department will process the application
3. Applicant will receive the certificate

4. Grant of license to manufacture insecticides
Eligibility: NA
Supporting Document:
1.Fee Paid challan
2.Registration Certificate issued by Central Insecticide Board & Registration Committee
3.Allotment letter/Rent agreement/ Lease agreement copy of the building (as applicable)
4.Building Plan of the Unit
5.List of Analytical Instrument in the Laboratory
6.Infrastructure facilities available in the unit
7.Local qualified doctor's acceptance letter for periodical health examination & during emergency
8.List of first aid measures adopted
9.Karnataka State Pollution Control Board Consent Letter
10.Municipal/ Panchayat license
11.Industries Dept. License (S. S. I.)
12.Memorandum and articles of Association/ Partnership deed/affidavit of Proprietorship on Rs. 100/- stamp paper and notarized
13.List of Directors/ Partners/Proprietor with age, fathers name and complete address
14.Appointment Order of chemist and expert staff
15.Acceptance Letter of chemist and expert staff
16.Marks cards and Degree certificates of chemists and expert staff
17.Affidavit with age, fathers name and complete address of chemists and expert staff and responsibility the quality control of the products manufactured on a Rs. 100/- stamp paper notarized
18.Safety measures undertaken in the unit
19.List of facilities extended to the workers in the unit (Wash rooms, change rooms, toilets etc.)
20.Specimen Signature 21.Passport size Photgraph 22.Authorization Letter/Nomination Letter
Application Fee : NA
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs 100
Delivery Time (Days) : 45
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant fills the form and attaches the annexures
2. Department will process the application
3. Applicant will receive the certificate
