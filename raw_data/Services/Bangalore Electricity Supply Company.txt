Department: Bangalore Electricity Supply Company

1. Name Change
Eligibility: Refer the document
Supporting Document:
1. Identity Proof
2. Ownership Proof
3. Latest Tax Paid Receipt or Katha Certificate
4. Consent Letter from the previous consumer (No Objection Certificate)
5. Indemnity Bond
6. Photocopy of the license or clearance issued in his favor by Local Authority if such license or Clearance is required under statute
7. Undertaking to pay an outstanding dues or short claims arise or detected at later stage, periods prior to the date of transfer
Application Fee : Rs.618
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs.20
Delivery Time (Days) : NA
Procedure for applying:
1. Submit the application with attachment & Fee Payment
2. The application shall be processed by BESCOM
3. Security Deposit, if any, is to be paid separately by the applicant
4. Applicant will receive information when the service is complete
