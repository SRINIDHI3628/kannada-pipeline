6. Intimation for payment of initial deposit and EMD
Eligibility: NA
Supporting Document:
1.Project clearance obtained from SHLCC/SLSWCC(if industry belongs to large/medium scale category)
2. Brief project Report
3. IT Returns
4. Land utilization sketch
5. Partnership deed
6. Copy of memorandum and article of association
7. License of Centre Gov. or registration/ letter of Intent report
8. Latest Communication receive from Govt. of India (If application for license is still pending)
Registration certificate/ Application For Registration (if industry has to be registered either as medium/small scale industries )
Application Fee : NA
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : NA
Delivery Time (Days) : 33 working days after receipt of SHLCC/SLSWCC/DLSWCC proceedings
Procedure for applying:
1 On filing the online application form along with the relevant documents and 30%/20%/10% tentative cost of land, land will be allotted on lease basis fixing the lease period of 99 years.
2. After payment of 100% tentative cost of land, confirmatory letter of allotment, and possession certificate of the plot will be issued.
3. Lease agreement is to be executed within 30 days from the date of receipt of Possession Certificate.
4. Allottees are required to complete the project and commence production within 36/60 months from the date of Possession Certificate.
