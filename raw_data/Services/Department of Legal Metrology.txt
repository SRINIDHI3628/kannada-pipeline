Deparment: Department of Legal Metrology

1. Issue Of New License To Manufacturer Of Weights & Measures
Eligibility: Indian Citizen
Supporting Document:
(1) Applicant(s) Permanent Address Certificate/Aadhaar Card/Ration Card/Passport/Tax Paid Receipt/Electrical Bill (Anyone)
(2) Applicant / Firm’s GST Certificate.
(3) Applicant(s) / Firms PAN Card.
(4) Manufacturer premise’s Rental Agreement / Lease Document / If own Tax Paid Receipt / Ownership Deed.
(5) Model Approval Certificate- Issuing by the Central Govt. of India.
(6) Partnership deed (in case of Partnership firm)
Application Fee : Rs.6000
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : NA
Delivery Time (Days) : 30 Working Days
Procedure for applying:
(1) Registration : Applicant registers on the E-Mapan Portal.
(2) Login : Login to the portal using the provided credentials.
(3) Service Selection : Select the desired service to be availed i.e. New License For Manufacturers Of Weights & Measures
(4) Application Form : Complete the application form by entering the required details. Upload a photo and signature of the applicant.
(5) Document Upload : Upload all necessary documents as specified.
(6) Online Payment : Make the payment through the Khajane 2 Portal.
(7) Submission : Submit the completed application form along with the uploaded documents and payment details.
(8) Inspection : The Inspecting Officer reviews and inspects the application. Officer provides remarks for the Approving Authority.
(9) Approval Process : The Approving Authority reviews the officer’s remarks. The application is either approved or rejected based on the review.
(10) Certificate Download : If approved, the applicant can download the certificate from their login.

Application Fee : Depends On The Items Being Verified & Stamped
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : NA
Delivery Time (Days) : 30 Working Days
Procedure for applying:
(1) Registration : Applicant registers on the E-Mapan Portal.
(2) Login : Login to the portal using the provided credentials.
(3) Service Selection : Select the service to be availed i.e. New/Renewal of Verification & Stamping Certificate.
(4) Item Details : Enter the details of items to be verified and stamped.
(5) Document Upload : Upload all necessary documents as specified.
(6) Online Payment : Make the payment through the Khajane 2 Portal.

(9) Approval or Rejection : Based on the verification, the application is either approved or rejected.
(10) Certificate Download : If Approved applicant can download the approved certificate & if Rejected applicant can download the rejection certificate from their login.
