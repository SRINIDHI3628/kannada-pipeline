Department: Backward Classes Welfare Department

1. Application for Caste Verification Report - OBC
Eligibility: Shortlisted canidadates from respective recuruting organisation
Supporting Document:
1. Primary school admission extract (1st Standard only).
2. Caste Certificate issued by tahsildar .
3. Passport Photo.
4. Caste certificate of father issued by Tahsildar from Permanent Address location (if Father's caste certificate not available then then father’s brother/ sister’s caste certificate).
5. Father’s School admission extract (if Father is illiterate then Father’s Brother/ sister’s school admission extract) .
6. Proof of employment of candidate and parents or/and spouse (For salaried employees consisting of Govt/Semi Govt/Private).
7. Previous 12 months salary slips issued by employer, As on date of application submission to Job notification.
8. RTC Certificate of all properties in candidates & parents/spouse name.
9. Land holding certificate/Bhu Hiduvali Patra issued by Tahsildar.
10. Landless certificate issued by Tahsildar.
11. Family tree.
12. Ration Card .
13. Photocopy of Aadhaar .
14. Family tree issued by Tahsildar.
Application Fee : NA
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs 35
Delivery Time (Days) : 15
Procedure for applying:
1. Respective recruting department to intimate the DC committee/District Backward Classes office and the candidate for the Sindhutva Pramana Patra.
2. Applicant to fill the form in the Seva Sindhu Portal.
3. District officer to verify the details/ documents and initiate field inspection by Taluk officer.
4. Taluk officer to conduct field inspection and submit report.
5. District officer to review the report, and set up meeting with DC committee.
6. DC committee to decide to issue Sindhutva Pramana Patra or not .

2. Post-matric Scholarship to BC Students
Eligibility: According to a notification issued by the Department
Supporting Document:
1. Caste And Income Certificate
2. Previous year’s passing Marks Card
3. Aadhar UID, if you have Aadhar Card
4. Ration Card Copy, if you have
5. Passport Size Photograph
6. Account should be opened in any nationalized bank which have got core banking facility. (name should be as in SSLC certificate)
Application Fee : NA
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : NA
Delivery Time (Days) : 365 Days
Procedure for applying:
1. Student should submit the online application and upload the necessary documents in the application.
2. Student should submit all the relevant Hard Copies of the documents to the studying College.
3. After the successful verification of the application department will sanction the amount to the student.
