1. Complaints related services
Eligibility: NA
Supporting Document:
NA
Application Fee : NA
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : 20
Delivery Time (Days) : 10
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant fills the form and registers a complaint

3. Applicant will receive information on the complaint resolution

2. Load Change
Eligibility: NA
Supporting Document:
Non Mandatory documents:
1. Sale Deed
2. Agreement
3. Copy of the latest Bill
4. Copy of the latest paid receipt
5. Consent letter to transfer the deposit
6. Any other document
Application Fee : You have to make payment of ₹100 in case of LT service or ₹200 in case of HT service at the CESC Sub-division office and submit the following documents.
(a) Agreement on ₹200 bond paper as per the format Annexure-07 in case of LT and Annexure-06 in case of HT installation.
(b) Completion/Test Report in the prescribed format obtained from the Licensed Electrical Contractor along with wiring diagram.
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : 20
Delivery Time (Days) : 30-180 days based on the subservice
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant fills the form and registers a request for Load Change
2. Department will process the application & applicant has to make payment of ₹100 in case of LT service or ₹200 in case of HT service at the CESC Sub-division office and submit the following documents.
(a) Agreement on ₹200 bond paper as per the format Annexure-07 in case of LT and Annexure-06 in case of HT installation.
(b) Completion/Test Report in the prescribed format obtained from the Licensed Electrical Contractor along with wiring diagram.
3. Applicant will receive information when the service is complete
