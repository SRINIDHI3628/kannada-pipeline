Department: Collegiate Education

1. Application for Admission extract
Eligibility: NA
Supporting Document:
1. ID proof
2. Fee payment receipt
Application Fee : Rs 0
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs 25
Delivery Time (Days) : 5
Procedure for applying:
1. Application submission (Online, B1/K1 centres, CSC centres, AJSK centres, BapujiSevaKendras)
2. The application is routed to Case Worker
3. Verification by the Case Worker. Recommendations of the Case worker are sent to the Superintendent for review
4. Verification by the Superintendent. Recommendations of the Superintendent are sent to the Principal for review
5. Review by the Principal. Approve or reject the application request.
