Inspection of Inland Vessels
Eligibility: NA
Supporting Document:
1. Certificate of Registry
2. Inspection Certificate
3. Competency Certificate of Master
4. Competency Certificate of Engine Driver
5. Crew List
6. Vessel Insurance
7. List of Life Savings equipments
8. List of fire fighting equipments
9. List of Navigation Light
10. Trim and Stability Certificate by Naval Architect
Application Fee : Based on GRT
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 30
Delivery Time (Days) : 15 days
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant fills the form and attaches the mandatory annexures
2. Department will process the application
3. Applicant will receive the certificate
