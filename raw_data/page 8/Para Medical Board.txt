Application for issue of Diploma Certificate
Eligibility: Student
Supporting Document:
1. All scanned marks card
2. SSLC marks card copy attested by gazette officer
3. PUC markscard copy attested by gazette officer
4. ID proof
5. Applicant Photo
6. Principal Declaration
Application Fee : 350
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : 25
Delivery Time (Days) : 15
Procedure for applying:
1.Application form submission by student
2.Verification by case worker
3.Application form approval/rejection by Member Secretary
