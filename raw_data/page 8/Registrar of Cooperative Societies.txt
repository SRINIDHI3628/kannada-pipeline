Registration of Souharda co-operative society
Eligibility: Citizens who intends to form the Co-operatives.
Supporting Document:
1. Copy of Permission to collect initial share capital
2. Bye Law
3. Form - 1 (Rule 3) information
4. Rental Agreement
5. Caste Certificates
6. Professional Certificates
7. Details of Shareholders (Name & Address, Age, Caste, No. of Shares, Share Amount, Signature)
8. Certificate/Extract from the bank for having remitted the prescribed share amount collected
9. Proceedings of promoters meeting
Application Fee : 1% of the total authorised share capital subject to below limits
Minimum - 500
Maximum - 5000
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 35
Delivery Time (Days) : 45
Procedure for applying:
1.After collection of the prescribed share capital amount, the chief promoter has to submit the proposal along with necessary documents at sakala counter.
2.Scrutinisation by the concerned case worker
3.Preparation of note in the file and submit to the superior authority.
4.Submission of file by concerned Suptd./CDO/ARCS as the case may be
5.The Registering authority after verification if found satisfactory will register the society and issue Registration Certificate.

1.Request letter to District Registrar.
2.Notices containing written or printed proposed amendment sent to members for attending special general meetings.
3.Postal acknowledgement of notice sent to its members for special general meetings/ General meetings.
4.Proceedings of two meetings.
5.Attendance of members present in the two meetings.
6.Certified copy of original Memorandum of Association (MOA) societies.
7.Amendment proposal.
8.Copy of latest returns filed.
Application Fee : As prescribed in rule 9 of the Karnataka societies registration rules 1961 Rs 100 (For SC/ST and Women societies 50 Percent of the fee prescribed) Rs 35 per page scanning fees
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 30
Delivery Time (Days) : 30
Procedure for applying:
1.The amendment proposal is to be submitted by the society at sakala counter.
2.Scrutinisation by the concerned case worker
3.Preparation of note in the file and submit to superintendent
4.After verification submit to District Registrar for registration of amendment.
5.The registering authority after due verification if the proposal found satisfactory will register the amendment and issue amendment certificate.

Registration of co-operative societies under Section 7 Of KCS Act, 1959
Eligibility: Citizens who intended to form the Co-operative Societies.
Supporting Document:
1. Copy of Permission to collect initial share capital
2. Bye Law
3. Schedule A
4. Rental Agreement
5. Caste Certificates
6. Professional Certificates
7. Details of Shareholders (Name & Address, Age, Caste, No. of Shares, Share Amount, Share Fees, Entry Fee, Total Amount, Signature)
8. Certificate/Extract from the bank for having remitted the prescribed share amount collected
9. Proceedings of promoters meeting
10. Project Report
Application Fee : Registration within Taluk Level Rs. 500
Above Taluk but within District Level Rs.1000
Above District but within State Level Rs. 2000
(If SC/ST, women or Backward classes, Minorities then 50% of the fees mentioned above)
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 35
Delivery Time (Days) : 45
Procedure for applying:
a) Application submission in on Seva Sindhu Portal along with submission of requisite documents
b) On the Basis of the Area of Operation, and Type of Society the application will be routed to the respective Assistant Registrar, Deputy Registrar, Joint Registrar of Additional Registrars Office
c) Application will be processed through Case worker, Office Superintendent, and respective officers based on Jurisdiction
d) Applicant to be initmated on the status of application
e) Payment receipt generation will be online through Seva Sindhu
