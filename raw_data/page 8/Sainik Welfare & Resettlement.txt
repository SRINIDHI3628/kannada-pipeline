Issue of Identity Card to Ex-servicemen
Eligibility: NA
Supporting Document:
1. Copy of Discharge Book/ Record of Services/ Service Particulars
2. Copy of Pension Payment Order (for pensioners)
3. Copy of Release Order and Gratuity Sanction letter for short service commissioned officers
4. Residential Proof
5. No Objection Certificate in case of residents of other District/State
Application Fee : For Officer - INR 150
For JCO - INR 100
For Other Rank - INR 50
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : 35
Delivery Time (Days) : 7
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant shall fill the form & upload necessary documents to apply for this service
2. The department shall process the valid application
3. Once the service is delivered, the applicant is notified
