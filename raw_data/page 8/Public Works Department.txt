Application to get Permission for Road Cutting on National Highways, State Highways and Major District Roads
Eligibility: NA
Supporting Document:
1. Address Proof/Authorization Letter from Company
Application Fee : NA
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : Rs. 100
Delivery Time (Days) : 15
Procedure for applying:
1. Applicant fills the form and attaches the mandatory annexures
2. Department will process the application
3. Applicant will be notified about the fee to be paid
4. Applicant makes the payment on sevasindhu
5. After verification of payment, applicant will receive the order copy

Contractor Registration / Renewal of Registration
Eligibility: One should have work experience related to civil and electrical as the case may be.
Supporting Document:
1) Solvency Certificate
2) Turnover Certificate
3) Photo
4) Contact details
5) Work Done certificate
6) GST Registration / returns details
Application Fee : For Fresh Registration
1) For Class-1 - Rs.10,000/- + Rs.500 (CBF)
2) For Class 2 - Rs. 5000/- + Rs.500 (CBF)
3) For Class 3 - Rs. 3000/- + Rs.500 (CBF)
4) For Class-4 - Rs. 2000/- + Rs.500 (CBF)
For Renewal of Registration
1) For Class-1 - Rs.5000/- + Rs.500 (CBF)
2) For Class 2 - Rs. 2000/- + Rs.500 (CBF)
3) For Class 3 - Rs. 1500/- + Rs.500 (CBF)
4) For Class-4 - Rs. 1000/- + Rs.500 (CBF)
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : NA
Delivery Time (Days) : 1) For Class-1 and 2 -30 days
2) For Class-3 - 20 days
3) For Class-4 - 10 days
Procedure for applying:
1) For Class-1 and 2 application shall be submitted to CE and approved by CE
2) For Class-3 application shall be submitted to SE and approved by SE
3) For Class-4 application shall be submitted to EE and approved by EE
