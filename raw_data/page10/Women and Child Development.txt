Mathrushree - Post natal Stage
Eligibility:
The beneficiary should be a
1. BPL card holder
2. Should be pregnant after March 2018
3. Applicable for only 2 living children
Supporting Document:
1. Photocopy of the Aadhaar of the beneficiary
2. Photocopy of the consent form duly signed by the beneficiary
Application Fee : 0
Service Charge (Free for Online Submission) : 0
Delivery Time (Days) : 0
Procedure for applying:
1. The DEO submits the application form on behalf of the applicant
2. The application is routed to Sanctioning Officer
3. Verification by the Sanctioning Officer. Recommendations of the Sanctioning Officer are sent to the State Nodal Officer for review
4. Review by the State Nodal Officer. Approve or reject the application request.
